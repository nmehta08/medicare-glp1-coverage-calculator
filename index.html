How the estimate was calculated

The calculator follows one cohort of Medicare Part D beneficiaries offered GLP-1 coverage for weight management. It assumes that 20% start treatment at the beginning of year one and that each starter fills one monthly prescription throughout the selected horizon.

Federal drug spending = people offered coverage × 20% uptake × 12 monthly fills per year × years × ($245 net price − $50 copay).

Worked example. 1,000,000 × 20% × 12 × 2 × $195 = $936,000,000.

Scope and interpretation

Estimates cover federal drug spending after beneficiary copays under the modeled payment terms. They exclude administrative expenses, clinical monitoring, adverse-event costs, potential medical savings, and spending under existing coverage. Estimating incremental Medicare budget impact would require a comparator and those additional costs.

Bridge eligibility depends on CMS clinical and coverage rules, including prior authorization and whether the prescribed use is already eligible for coverage under Part D. Use CMS provider guidance for individual coverage decisions. [5]

Published benchmark. Applying the same arithmetic to KFF’s 18-month example, with 3.8 million eligible beneficiaries, 10%–25% uptake, and $195 per monthly fill, gives about $1.3–$3.3 billion in federal drug spending. [4]
